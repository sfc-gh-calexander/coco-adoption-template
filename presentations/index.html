What both versions cover

A generic demo does not drive adoption. A build tied to the customer's actual data does. This template removes the friction between "I have a meeting tomorrow" and "I have something worth showing."

01 · Why It Matters

Why account-specific builds outperform generic demos, and how to make the case to a skeptical stakeholder. Read the case →

02 · Account Discovery

Three questions that map a customer conversation directly to a build pattern. Walk in prepared, not guessing.

03 · 30-Minute Setup

From cold start to demo-ready. Connect, identify tables, define one business question, build the agent, app, or pipeline that answers it.

04 · Vertical Patterns

Production patterns for Financial Services, Restaurant, Healthcare, and SaaS. Adapt, don't start from zero. See patterns →

05 · Objection Handling

The five objections every SE encounters, including "We already have Copilot" and "My data team won't trust it."

06 · AE Handoff Card

What the demo answered, three follow-up questions, and the expansion story. The AE runs the next session without the SE.

About this template

Built from real-world production deployments. Part of Snowflake's SE adoption resource library. Dual-format structure: business case for decision makers, hands-on lab for builders.

The hands-on lab and vertical patterns are backed by actual account builds. Each pattern is anonymized - the patterns are real, the names are not.

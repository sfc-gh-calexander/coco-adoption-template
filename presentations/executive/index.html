The problem

The problem with generic demos

Most CoCo sessions follow the same pattern: impressive demo on fictional data, customer is interested, follow-up never happens.

  • Generic data cannot be mapped to the customer's actual workflow
  • After the demo, the AE has nothing specific to follow up on
  • Production becomes a separate project, separately scoped and budgeted
  • The account stays interested but not committed
The reframe

What CoCo actually enables

CoCo is an AI coding agent that lives inside Snowflake. It reads your data, writes code against it, and builds applications within your governance boundary.

An SE can connect to a customer's Snowflake environment and build an AI agent that answers a business question using their actual data in under 30 minutes. That is not a demo. That is production.

The pattern

Three questions, one build

1

Ask three discovery questions

What decision takes too long? Where is that data? What would you ask it in plain English?

2

Connect to their environment

Their actual Snowflake tables. No sandbox, no sample data.

3

Build in 30 minutes

Semantic view plus agent wired to their data. Answers the question they gave you.

4

Hand off to the AE

One-page card: what the demo answered, three follow-up questions, the expansion story.

12 additional AI workstreams across the organization, each catalyzed by a single SE-delivered CoCo session.
Vertical pattern

Financial Services ISV

What was built
A geospatial analytics agent embedded in the ISV's product. Their customers interact with spatial data through natural language, powered by Snowflake.
What it unlocked
Every customer of the ISV gets natural-language access to spatial data through the ISV's own product. One deployment scales to the ISV's entire customer base without each one building anything separately.
Vertical pattern

What was built
A governed clinical query agent for a healthcare data platform. Non-technical clinical staff query PHI-adjacent data safely, with governance controls at the CoCo layer.
What it unlocked
A reusable pattern for regulated healthcare accounts. The governance setup is documented and repeatable. New accounts deploy in one session.

What it unlocked
